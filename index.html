Why We Started

Restocking shelves is the job nobody in grocery retail wants. It is heavy, repetitive, and back-breaking, and it is the single hardest role for stores to keep staffed. We know how to solve it with a robotic system — and that is why we are building AILE Robotics.

80% of stores report labour shortages
60% annual employee turnover
46% of store hours spent restocking

What We're Building

Robots That See and Reason

Perception, planning, and control that let a robot handle real shelves — damaged trays, unstable products, and messy aisles included.

One Brain, Many Robots

A central model coordinates the fleet, so every shift in every store makes the whole system better.

Humans in the Loop

When a robot gets stuck, a person steps in — and that correction becomes training data. The system compounds from day one.
